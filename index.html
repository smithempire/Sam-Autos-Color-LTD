About the company

Built on colour, trusted for finish

SAM AUTOS COLOR LIMITED imports, distributes and sells automotive paints and refinishing supplies to body shops, panel beaters, dealerships and independent sprayers across Nigeria. From our base in Onitsha, Anambra State, we stock everything a professional refinish job requires — paints, primers, clears, hardeners, thinners, fillers, abrasives and the equipment to apply them.

We work directly with established paint brands and mixing systems, so every can that leaves our store is genuine, correctly labelled and ready for accurate colour matching on the shop floor.

  • Genuine, verified stock — no counterfeits
  • Full refinish range under one roof
  • Trade pricing for body shops & dealers
  • Same-day pickup, nationwide delivery
